# T17 — Sprites for the remaining and future simple components

Status: ready_for_fukit

## Dependencies

[T16 — 8-bit component sprites](T16-component-sprites.md). Its acceptance criteria must be satisfied before starting this task.

## Outcome and commit boundary

Give every existing catalog kind an 8-bit sprite, and record approved designs for common simple (non-programmable) breadboard parts that the catalog does not have yet, so each can get a sprite as soon as its electrical model exists.

Suggested commit title: `feat: draw remaining components as 8-bit sprites`.

The deliverable must stand on its own at this milestone. Do not include unrelated refactors or publication steps.

## Scope and interfaces

Follow [the sprite design reference](../design/sprites/README.md): its style rules, palette, and "Adding a component sprite" steps. Bodies stay smaller than their pin span wherever possible so legs and holes stay visible.

### Part A — implement: existing kinds without art

| Kind | Pins (axis first) | Body idea | States |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `capacitor` | `positive` → `negative` | Top-down electrolytic can: blue ring, lighter stripe with a minus mark on the negative side, silver top with a vent cross. About 12 x 12 px. | 1 |
| `npn_transistor` | `emitter` → `collector` across, `base` in the middle | TO-92 seen from above: black D shape with the flat face toward the viewer and three legs. About 14 x 8 px. | 1 |
| `changeover_switch` | `normally_closed` → `normally_open`, `common` in the middle | Black slide switch body with a light slider knob. About 14 x 8 px. | 2: slider toward NC or toward NO, from the core control state |
| `dc_voltage_source` | `positive`, `negative` | Off-board supply block: dark case, red + and blue − terminals, "5 V" marking in pixels. Keep the existing off-board position and colored supply wires. | 1 |

- Three-pin parts need `sprites::place` to support a middle pin: the leads still run from every pin's hole to the body, and the axis stays the two outer pins. Add this generally; do not special-case one kind in `main.rs`.
- Remove each implemented kind from the plain fallback. If every kind has art, keep the fallback path only if it is still reachable; otherwise delete it.

### Part B — design only: future simple parts

Add a "Future parts" section to the design reference with an approved design for each part below: body size, pins and axis, states, palette additions, and a short visual description. Add golden `.txt` references generated by a test-only function, but no `PartArt` registration, no new `ComponentKind`, and no project format change. Adding a kind needs its own electrical model and card.

| Part | Pins | Body idea | States |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rectifier diode (1N4007) | anode → cathode | Black cylinder with a silver cathode band, the same length as the resistor. | 1 |
| Signal diode (1N4148) | anode → cathode | Orange glass body with a black cathode band. | 1 |
| Ceramic capacitor | a → b | Small ochre disc on two legs. | 1 |
| Potentiometer (trimmer) | end a → end b, wiper in the middle | Blue square with a white cross-slot knob. | 1 (knob angle may come later from a parameter) |
| Photoresistor (LDR) | a → b | Round orange-red disc with a zigzag track. | 1 |
| PNP transistor | emitter → collector, base in the middle | Same TO-92 body as the NPN; tell them apart by label and hover text, not by a different shape. | 1 |
| LED colors | anode → cathode | Green, yellow, and blue palettes for the existing LED body, each with off, dim, and lit shades. | 3 each |
| Slide switch (SPDT) and toggle switch | as the changeover switch | Variants of the Part A switch body. | 2 |

Excluded: parts that need programming or protocol behavior (microcontrollers, sensors with digital output, displays, drivers) and parts that need new physics beyond simple two- or three-terminal models (motors, relays, speakers). These need separate specifications.

The core is not changed. Hole geometry, connectivity, and electrical results are unchanged.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Capacitor, NPN transistor, changeover switch, and voltage source are drawn as sprites that follow the style rules; their bodies match committed golden references.
- [ ] Placement supports three-pin parts through the shared placement code; every lead ends in its pin hole for arbitrary valid layouts (fixed-seed property test covering two- and three-pin parts).
- [ ] The changeover switch sprite follows the core control state; tests cover both positions and stale readings not changing it.
- [ ] All embedded fixtures (LED, RC, transistor) render with no fallback rectangles left for supported kinds.
- [ ] Every Part B design is documented in the design reference with a golden reference; none is registered as a runtime kind.
- [ ] New palette colors have unique legend symbols; the legend test passes.
- [ ] Existing app and sprite tests still pass.
